## 2. Our story

**Partners in life. Partners in frame.**

NxA is a creative studio in Dubai run by two artists. Navid is a photographer and art director who sees in light, lines and space. Ava is a filmmaker, storyteller, cook and stylist who sees in people, flavour and feeling. Together we make places, products and brands look the way they deserve to look — on camera, by hand, with no shortcuts.

**Why NxA exists:** Navid is an established architectural photographer and Ava has her own creative practice. Both wanted to try new fields outside their main industries without putting everything in one place. NxA is that shared space: Dubai plus media, across architecture, design, food and beverage.

| | Navid — the eye | Ava — the voice |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Photographer & art director | Filmmaker, storyteller, cook & stylist |
| Territory | Space, architecture, interiors, light | Food, people, styling, story |
| Energy | Calm, precise | Energetic, warm |
| On camera | Behind it — shy and quiet; seen through frames, hands and process | In front of it — the voice of the brand |

## 3. The big idea

# His frame. *Her story.*

Navid builds the frame: light, lines, space and material. Ava tells the story: people, flavour and feeling. Most small businesses get only one of the two — a nice photo with no story, or a story with no eye — and big agencies cost more than a company under 50 employees can pay. NxA gives you both in one frame, made by hand and camera, at a price a small business can pay.

Everything in the identity serves this one idea.

## 4. Mission, vision, values

**Mission:** Make Dubai's independent places look as good as they really are.

**Vision:** The studio Dubai's best small brands name when someone asks: *"who shot this?"*

**Values**

1. **Made by hand** — Real people, real cameras, real light. No AI faces, no AI voices.
2. **Tailored, not templated** — Every client gets their own cut. No copy-paste looks.
3. **Affordable luxury** — Omega, not Patek Philippe, not Swatch. Premium a small business can own.
4. **Two eyes, one frame** — Space and story, always shot together. Navid's frame, Ava's feeling.

## 5. Positioning

**Statement:** For independent restaurants, cafés, boutique hotels and design brands in Dubai that want to look world-class, NxA is the two-artist studio that tailors space, food and story into one look — at a price a small business can pay. Unlike volume agencies, you always know who is behind the camera.

**What's different:** Two named artists, not a faceless agency. One team covers space (Navid) and food, styling and story (Ava). Dubai's F&B agencies sell volume marketing; solo studios cover one craft. NxA is art-led, multidisciplinary and personal.

**Why believe it:** Navid's award-recognised architectural and interior photography, past boutique-hotel and hospitality work, Ava's food and Persian culinary depth, and real hands and real cameras on every job.

**Points of view**
1. Made by hand and camera, by real people.
2. Looking expensive is a decision, not a budget.
3. A space, a plate and a person are one story — shoot them together.

**Positioning map** (craft-led ↔ volume-led × named artists ↔ faceless)

| | Volume-led | Craft-led |
|---|---|---|
| **Named artists** | DIY / freelancers | **NxA** |
| **Faceless** | Big F&B agencies | Solo specialists (middle) |

**Tagline line:** *Not Patek. Not Swatch. Omega.*

**Unique angle to own:** Persian food + luxury spaces. Ava runs @persianculinary; Ariana's Persian Kitchen (inside Atlantis The Royal) won the MICHELIN Guide Dubai's first Opening of the Year award. No one else in Dubai owns this niche.

## 7. Audience

### Lead segment (months 1–3): Hospitality owners
- **Who:** Owners and managers of independent restaurants, cafés, boutique hotels and concept stores in Dubai, under 50 staff.
- **Want:** To look as good as the big names and win bookings, without a big-agency bill.

- Interior studios, furniture and design brands, developer show units, fashion and lifestyle labels.

Atlantis The Royal · Ariana's Persian Kitchen · Studio Nada Debs · Omniyat · The Lana Dubai.
These set the visual level. The paying clients are their smaller neighbours: **the big-hotel look at a price a small business can pay.**

## 9. Voice and tone

**Who speaks:** Ava is the on-camera voice. Navid is the eye behind the camera — seen through his frames, hands and process. Being shy on camera is part of the character.

**Dimensions:** Formality 2/5 · Warmth 4/5 · Authority 4/5 · Energy 3.5/5 · Humour 3/5 · Polish 4/5

| We sound like… | …never |
|---|---|
| a friend with taste | a sales deck |
| confident — we show the work | boastful |
| futuristic & minimal | cold |
| playful in the studio | careless with clients |

**Words we use:** tailored · frame · light · texture · table · space · story · hand-made · the scene

**Words we ban:** elevate · next level · game-changer · unlock · premium quality content · in today's fast-paced world · fake urgency

**Devices:** Short lines. Real process over claims. Emoji: one at most, never as bullets. Hashtags: 3–5 topic tags, never a block. Sign off with ×.

**Sample caption**
> Same café. Same table. Same coffee.
> We just changed the light, the angle and the story.
> His frame. Her story. ×

**Pronunciation:** NxA = "N-ex-A" · Navid = "na-VEED" · Ava = "AH-va"

## 10. Logo

**Meaning:** N for Navid. A for Ava. × for what happens when they work together.

**Construction — the 35° line**
- Every diagonal in the mark (N, ×, A) runs at **35°**. It is the brand's signature line.
- **1 unit** = cap height of N and A.
- **½ unit** = height of the ×, which sits on the optical centre between the letters.
- The letters stay quiet in Ink; the × carries the colour.

**Variations**

| Version | Letters | × | Use on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Ink | Damask Rose | Porcelain or Titanium |
| Reversed | Porcelain | Damask Rose | Ink |
| On Rose | Porcelain | Ink | Damask Rose — statements |
| Mono Ink | Ink | Ink | Print, stamps, emboss |
| Mono Porcelain | Porcelain | Porcelain | Over dark photos |
| The × | — | Rose / Ink / Porcelain | Avatar, favicon, sign-off |

**Clear space:** on every side = the height of the ×.
**Minimum size:** wordmark 120 px digital / 30 mm print. Below that, or inside a circle, use the × alone.
**Instagram avatar:** the Rose × on Ink. Never the full wordmark in a circle.

**Never:** stretch or squash · recolour the × · add shadows or effects · rotate · use Primary on Rose (the × disappears) · retype it in a font.

## 11. Colour

**Quiet base. One spark.** Porcelain and Titanium replaced Bone and Dubai Sand in October 2026: the warm creams read too close to Claude's own colours, and the cool pair is more futuristic.

| Name | HEX | RGB | Use | Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Porcelain | `#F4F4F2` | 244 244 242 | Main background, paper | 45% |
| Ink | `#111111` | 17 17 17 | Letters, text, night frames | 30% |
| Titanium | `#C9CDD4` | 201 205 212 | Cool surfaces, cards | 12% |
| Damask Rose | `#D62271` | 214 34 113 | The ×, accents — one per frame | 8% |
| Slate | `#7D838D` | 125 131 141 | Secondary text, lines | 3% |
| Blush | `#F4D6E2` | 244 214 226 | Soft highlights, Ava's side | 2% |

| Base | Light · mid · grey | Use for |

## 13. Typography

**Shout, whisper, speak.** All free Google Fonts — the team, freelancers and clients can all use them.

| Role | Font | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Display — the shout | **Syne** ExtraBold / Bold | Futuristic, artful, wide. Short headlines only. |
| Accent — the whisper | **Instrument Serif** Italic | The story. One line per layout, next to the shout. |
| Body — the speaking voice | **Manrope** Regular / Bold | Body copy, captions; Bold caps for labels. |

**Scale (Figma text styles)**

| Style | Font | Size / line height / tracking |
|---|---|---|
| Display/XL | Syne ExtraBold | 160 / 95% / −3 px |
| Display/L | Syne ExtraBold | 96 / 100% / −2 px |
| Heading/H1 | Syne Bold | 56 / 110% / −1 px |
| Heading/H2 | Syne Bold | 32 / 120% / −0.5 px |
| Accent/Serif L | Instrument Serif Italic | 72 / 105% / −1 px |
| Accent/Serif M | Instrument Serif Italic | 40 / 115% |
| Body/L | Manrope Regular | 24 / 150% |
| Body/M | Manrope Regular | 18 / 155% |
| Label/Caps | Manrope Bold, uppercase | 14 / 130% / +18% |

**Rules**
- Headlines: sentence case, max 6 words, Syne.
- One serif italic line per layout — never two.
- Body: 45–75 characters per line.
- Instagram (1080 px canvas): headline 72–96, body 32–40, never below 28.
- Never stretch, outline or shadow type.

**Arabic & Persian companions:** IBM Plex Sans Arabic · Vazirmatn — for bilingual posts and Persian food stories.

**Alternative pairings**

| Direction | Display + Body | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| More classic | Fraunces + Inter | Hotels, fine dining |
| More editorial | Playfair Display Italic + Space Grotesk | Fashion, styling |
| More futuristic | Unbounded + Manrope | Launches, events |

## 17. Film and Reels

**She talks. He frames.**

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Open on the result — the finished plate, the lit room. On-screen words match spoken words. |
| Ava | On camera, talking to one person. Warm, quick, real. Never read off a phone. |
| Navid | Behind the camera. Seen through hands, process and the final frame. |
| Captions | Always burned in: Manrope Bold, Porcelain on Ink at 70%, lower third, max 2 lines. |
| The × wipe | Signature transition: a 35° diagonal wipe from before to after. |
| End card | The × on Ink, 1 second, no music sting. |

## 18. Graphic devices

**Small tools, used sparingly.**

- **The 35° line** — dividers, wipes, crops. Always at the logo's angle.
- **The × stamp** — sign-off on posts, avatar, watermark at 8% opacity.
- **Frame corners** — crop marks around a hero shot: the photographer's view.
- **Before → after split** — before/after on one frame, cut on the 35° line.

## 21. Rules and AI policy

**Real hands. Real cameras.**

| Always | Never |
|---|---|
| Show the process — hands, set-ups, light | AI faces, AI voices or cloned voices |
| Name the people behind every shoot | AI-generated food, spaces or people as our work |
| Get client approval before posting their work | Fake numbers, fake urgency or fake reviews |
| Burn captions into every video | Name or mock other agencies |
| Write alt text for every image | Post client work before they publish |
| Respect UAE content rules and guests' privacy | Stock photos |

**AI policy:** AI may help us plan, draft captions and edit. It never replaces the camera. Every portfolio image is a real shoot.

**UAE sensitivity:** modest styling; no alcohol-forward content unless the venue and licence allow it; get permission before filming staff or guests.
