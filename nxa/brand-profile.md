## Identity
- **What it is (one sentence):** NxA is a Dubai creative studio run by two artists, Navid (photographer and art director) and Ava (filmmaker, storyteller, cook and stylist), who make hospitality and design businesses look tailored on camera.
- **Name:** N × A = Navid × Ava, the collaboration of two people. Said "N-ex-A" or "N by A"; the × is the brand.
- **Category:** Boutique content and creative studio: photo, film, styling and art direction.
- **Founded / location:** 2026, Dubai. Founders are partners in work and life.
- **Stage:** Brand new, 0 followers, no NxA social channels yet. Draft logo exists (`nxa/assets/logo-draft.svg`).

## Positioning
- **The metaphor (core idea):** Most small businesses show up online in a t-shirt and hoodie. NxA puts them in a well-cut suit.
- **What's different:** Two named artists, not a faceless agency. One team covers **space** (Navid: architecture and interiors) and **food, styling and story** (Ava). Dubai F&B agencies sell volume marketing. Solo studios cover one craft. NxA is art-led, multidisciplinary and personal.
- **Price position:** Affordable luxury. Omega, not Patek Philippe and not Swatch. The big-hotel look at a price a company with under 50 staff can pay.
- **Why believe it:** Navid's award-recognised architectural and interior photography, past boutique hotel and hospitality work, Ava's food and Persian culinary depth, and real hands and real cameras on every job.
- **Point of view:**
  1. Made by hand and camera, by real people. No AI faces, no AI voices.
  2. Looking expensive is a decision, not a budget.
  3. A space, a plate and a person are one story, so shoot them together.
- **Won't say:** "Best agency in Dubai", "100x your sales", or any claim we can't show in a frame.

## Audience
### Segment 1: Hospitality owners (lead segment for months 1–3)
- **Who:** Owners and managers of independent restaurants, cafés, boutique hotels and concept stores in Dubai, with under 50 staff.
- **Cares about:** Looking as good as the big names, getting bookings, standing out on a feed full of the same flat-lay.
- **Believes / objects:** "Good content is either cheap phone stuff or an agency I can't afford." "Every agency says they're premium."
- **Where:** Instagram first. Decides fast, by look and trust.
- **Transformation:** T-shirt to suit. Generic or DIY content becomes a tailored visual identity that matches the quality of the place.
### Segment 2: Design, interiors and lifestyle brands (secondary)
- **Who:** Interior studios, furniture and design brands, developers' show units, fashion and lifestyle labels.
- **Buyer vs follower note:** Followers will include creatives and design lovers. Buyers are owners. Content must impress the first and convert the second.
### Aspiration set (the bar, not the price list)
- Atlantis The Royal, Ariana's Persian Kitchen, Studio Nada Debs, Omniyat, The Lana Dubai. These define the visual level NxA aims for. The paying clients are their smaller neighbours.

## Voice
- **Dimensions:** Formality 2/5 · Warmth 4/5 · Authority 4/5 · Energy 3.5/5 (calm Navid × energetic Ava) · Humor 3/5 · Polish 4/5
- **We sound ___, never ___:**
  - Like a friend with impeccable taste, never like a sales deck.
  - Confident because we show the work, never boastful.
  - Futuristic and minimal, never cold.
  - Playful in the studio, sharp in the meeting.
- **Who speaks:** Ava is the on-camera voice and story. Navid is the eye behind the camera, seen through his frames, hands and process; he is shy and quiet on camera, and that is part of the character.
- **Words we use:** tailored, frame, light, texture, table, space, story, hand-made, "the suit".
- **Words we ban:** "elevate", "next level", "game-changer", "unlock", "premium quality content", "in today's fast-paced world", fake urgency.
- **Signature devices:** Short lines. Real process over claims. Emoji at most one, never as bullets. Hashtags: 3–5 topic tags, never a block.
- **Language:** English. A touch of Persian warmth (food, hospitality) where it's natural.
- **Pronunciation guide:** NxA = "N-ex-A". Navid = "na-VEED". Ava = "AH-va".

- **Shoot rates:** natrvash.com/uae

## Goals (3 months)
- 10k followers · 2–3 qualified enquiries a month · 1 signed client a month.

## Operational defaults
- **AI & synthetic media policy:** No AI avatars, no AI or cloned voices, no AI-generated people or food passed off as real work. AI may help with planning, copy drafts and editing tools only. Every portfolio image is a real shoot.
- **Accessibility defaults:** Alt text on every image. Burned-in captions on every video, since most people watch muted.
- **Visual identity:** See `nxa/brand-kit.md` (to be built in design-and-templates).

## Guardrails
- **Competitor rules:** Never name or mock other agencies. Win by showing, not comparing.
- **Client rules:** Never post client work before the client has published or approved it.
- **Sensitivity:** Respect UAE content rules: modest styling, no alcohol-forward content unless the venue and licence allow it, and get permission before filming staff or guests.
