## 1. Daily engagement routine (30 min)

| Block | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Morning (15 min)** | 9:00–9:15 | 1. Reply to every DM; send `/scene` to new "SCENE" messages. 2. Leave **5 thoughtful comments**: 3 on target venues from the outreach list, 2 on Dubai design, food or architecture accounts. 3. Reply to yesterday's late comments. |
| **After posting (15 min)** | The first hour after each post (posts go out ~7:30–9 pm) | 1. Reply to **every** comment in the first hour, ideally with a question so the thread grows. 2. Share the post to stories with the 35° line sticker. 3. Like and answer story replies. |

On days without a post, use the evening block for stories and for replying to story replies.

**Who does what (suggestion):** Ava answers comments and story replies, since she's the voice. Navid handles DMs that turn into business, WhatsApp, and the outreach list.

### Comment rules
- Specific, about something real in their post: "That window light on the marble is beautiful — what time was this?" Never "Nice 🔥".
- Never pitch in comments. The pitch happens in DMs, after warming up (strategy §8).
- Voice rules apply: short, warm, no hype words, one emoji at most.
- Never use automation tools, follow/unfollow, engagement pods or bought followers.

## 4. Weekly report with Claude (Sunday, 10 minutes)

Every Sunday, send me these screenshots from Instagram (Professional dashboard → Insights):

1. **Account overview (last 7 days):** accounts reached, accounts engaged, followers gained.
2. **Profile activity:** profile visits and external link taps.
3. **Top content:** posts and Reels sorted by **reach**, then by **shares**, then by **saves**.
4. For each Reel: **average watch time** and **skip rate** (or the retention graph).
5. A count of "SCENE" DMs and WhatsApp conversations that week (just tell me the number).

**What I send back:** what worked and why, one change for next week's content, and updates to the calendar.

## 5. What to watch, and what to do about it

These are starting benchmarks for a new account. I'll adjust them after the first 4 weeks of real data.

| Signal | Where | If it's low | Do this |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Skip rate / first 3 seconds** | Reel insights | Most people swipe away in the first 3 s | Start on the result (the finished room, the plate), put text on frame 1, cut the intro. |
| **Average watch time** | Reel insights | Under about half the Reel's length | Shorten the Reel, cut slow middle shots, keep one idea per Reel. |
| **Shares (sends)** | Post insights | Few shares relative to reach | Make it more useful or more surprising: a before/after, a tip, or a strong opinion people forward. |
| **Saves** | Post insights | Few saves | Add "save this" content: lists, tips, step-by-step carousels. |
| **Profile visits → follows** | Profile insights | Many visits, few follows | Fix the profile: clearer bio, a stronger pinned post, a complete grid. |
| **Link taps / SCENE DMs** | Profile insights, DMs | Reach grows but no enquiries | Add a clear "DM 'SCENE'" to 1 in 5 posts and make the Book highlight clearer. |

**Pinned posts (3):** #1 "We're Navid & Ava", Mise-en-scène No.01, and the best-performing post of the month (swap it monthly).

## 6. If a Reel goes viral (Q4)

A Reel is "taking off" when it reaches several times your follower count, or several times your usual Reel reach, within 48 hours.

**Within the first hour**
1. Reply to every comment you can, with questions to keep threads going.
2. Pin the 2–3 best comments.
3. Share it to stories with a question sticker ("What should we shoot next?").

**Within 24 hours**
4. Answer **every** "SCENE" DM first; move serious ones to WhatsApp the same day.
5. Pin the Reel to the profile.
6. Check the Book highlight is up to date and the link works.

**Within 48 hours**
7. Post a **Part 2** in the same format (same hook style, same length, a new place or angle). Name it so it links back: "Mise-en-scène No.0X — part 2" or "You asked about…".
8. Answer the 3 most common questions from the comments in stories or a short Reel.

**Don't**
- Don't change the look to chase the new audience.
- Don't post a hard sale on top of it. The work sells; the DMs follow.

## 7. Monthly review (last Sunday)

1. Followers vs target (1,000 · 4,000 · 10,000 stretch).
2. The 3 posts that brought the most **follows** and **shares**, and what they have in common.
3. Which pillar performs best → move one weekly slot towards it.
4. Outreach funnel: warmed up → DM'd → replied → call → shoot.
5. **End of month 1 only:** the ads decision. Boost the 1–2 best Reels with about 500–1,000 AED, targeted at business owners in Dubai, only if they clearly beat the rest organically.
6. Update the calendar for next month.
