## Music and sound

- **Default:** calm, minimal electronic under the **real sound of the place** (cups, steps, cutlery, the shutter). Mix the music low (about −18 dB under the ambient) so the place leads.
- **Ava talking:** music very low or off, so her voice carries.
- **Trend Sunday:** the trending audio of the week.
- **Business accounts:** Instagram limits Business accounts mostly to its royalty-free commercial library, so many trending songs are not available. Two options:
  1. Use the commercial library (in Reels → audio). Many calm electronic tracks are there.
  2. If a trend needs a song that isn't available, switch the account to **Creator** (you keep Insights and the contact button) or use a licensed library such as Epidemic Sound or Artlist.
- **Never** use a song you don't have the rights to on client work.
