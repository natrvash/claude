# NxA — Month 1: Every Post in Detail
<!-- version 1.0 · 2026-10-27 · month 1 = Mon 2 Nov – Sun 29 Nov 2026 · this file replaces the scripts in ../instagram/launch-9.md -->

This is the production file for all 16 posts of month 1. Each post has: the idea, two hook options, the script, a shot list, on-screen text, sound, cover, caption, hashtags, alt text and the stories that go with it.

## Before you start

**House rules (from the brand book)**
- Slogan: *His frame. Her story.* Before/after line: *Same place. New scene.*
- Ava speaks; Navid is seen through his hands, the camera and the light, and answers in his own words.
- The script gives the meaning. Say it in your own words; never read it off a phone.
- On-screen text: Manrope Bold, Porcelain on Ink at 70%, lower third, 2 lines max. The first frame always has text.
- Every Reel ends on the 1-second end card: the Rose × on Ink, no music sting.
- Sound: calm electronic, mixed low under the real sound of the place. Ava's voice always wins.
- **DM 'SCENE'** appears on only 3 of the 16 posts (#5, #10, #14). The rest just tell the story.
- Fill every `[bracket]` before filming. Get written permission from every venue before posting.

**Hashtag sets (3–5 per post, rotate)**
- **A · Interiors:** #dubaiinteriors #interiorphotography #architecturephotography #dubaidesign
- **B · Cafés & food:** #dubaicafe #dubaifoodphotography #foodstyling #dubaieats
- **C · Studio:** #dubaiphotographer #dubaivideographer #behindthescenes #madebyhand

**Shoot batches**
- **Batch 1 · Sat 31 Oct:** #1, #3, #4, #7. One bright location (your home or a friend's café), one afternoon, 3–4 hours.
- **Archive · Sun 1 Nov:** #2, #5, #6 from the No.01 project files.
- **Batch 2 · Sat 7 Nov:** #11, #15 and spare Behind-the-frame questions.
- **No.02 shoot · by Thu 12 Nov:** #8, #9 (partly), #10.
- **No.03 shoot · by Sun 22 Nov:** #13 (partly), #14, #16.
