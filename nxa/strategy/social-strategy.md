## 1. The plan in one paragraph

For the first 3 months NxA builds a **portfolio on Instagram (@nxa.uae)** and treats **follower growth as the scoreboard**. Every week there are 4 posts and daily stories across three pillars: Ava's stories, the **Mise-en-scène** makeover series, and Behind the frame. We get the first projects through a mix of spec makeovers, free collabs and founding clients. Every week we reach out warmly to 5 venues, and interested owners go from a **DM keyword to WhatsApp**. Paid ads are decided after month 1.

## 4. Content pillars

| Pillar | What it is | Format | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Ava's stories** | Ava on camera telling stories about past work, ideas and process | Reels, 30–60 s | Trust, personality, follows |
| **2 · Mise-en-scène No.XX** | T-shirt → suit makeovers of real Dubai places (spec, collab or client) | Reel reveal + before/after carousel | Proof, shares, enquiries |
| **3 · Behind the frame** | Ava interviews Navid: one question per Reel, behind the scenes | Reels, 20–45 s | The craft, the couple, the "real hands" promise |

**Ava's formats by topic (Q13)**
- **Past work** → walk-and-talk on location.
- **Ideas and opinions** → seated talking head in a beautiful corner, with b-roll cut in.
- **Process** → talking while styling or setting up a shot, with Navid filming.

**Behind the frame (Q14).** Ava asks one short question and Navid answers in a sentence or two. His hands, the camera and the light carry the rest. Example questions:
- "Why did you move that lamp?"
- "What's the first thing you look at in a room?"
- "Phone or camera for a café?"
- "What makes food look expensive?"
- "Your favourite light in Dubai?"

**Mise-en-scène series rules**
- Title: `Mise-en-scène No.01 — [Venue name]`, numbered forever (No.01, No.02…).
- Structure: the place as it shows itself today (t-shirt) → the 35° × wipe → NxA's version (suit) → one line on what changed.
- Always tag the venue. Spec makeovers only with the owner's permission.

## 5. Weekly rhythm

UAE weekend is Saturday–Sunday.

| Day | Post | Pillar |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Ava's story Reel | 1 |
| **Wednesday** | Mise-en-scène No.XX — Reel reveal (+ before/after carousel when there's enough material) | 2 |
| **Friday** | Behind the frame — Ava interviews Navid | 3 |
| **Sunday** | Trend of the week in NxA style (a pillar dressed on a trending sound or format) | Rotating |
| **Every day** | Stories: behind the scenes, polls, question box, "today's light", reposts of tags | All |

**Trend rule (Q16):** one trend a week, always in the NxA look (Porcelain/Ink/Rose, Syne + serif, 35° wipe). Never a meme format that doesn't fit the brand.

## 8. Outreach routine (Q9)

**Each week, 5 venues** from the target list: independent restaurants, cafés, boutique hotels and concept stores, under 50 staff.

1. **Monday — warm up:** follow, then leave 2–3 thoughtful comments on their recent posts (never "nice pic").
2. **Wednesday — DM:** a short, personal note plus one specific makeover idea for their place.
3. **In person:** visit 1–2 a week with the business card and a printed before/after.
4. Log every venue in a simple tracker: name, handle, date, stage (warm → DM → reply → call → shoot).

**DM template (adapt every time)**
> Hi [name] — we're Navid & Ava from NxA. We love [specific detail of their place]. We had an idea for how [their dish/room] could look on camera: [one line]. Would you be open to us trying it? ×

## 9. Lead path (Q7)

1. Posts and stories end with **"DM 'SUIT'"** (on sales posts only, max 1 in 5).
2. Instant reply (saved reply): "Thank you! Three quick questions: ① your venue and Instagram, ② what you need: a shoot, monthly content or a new look, ③ when?"
3. After the answers → "Let's continue on WhatsApp: [number]". Send rates (natrvash.com/uae) and book a call or visit.
4. Tracker stage: DM → WhatsApp → call/visit → proposal → signed.

## 11. Paid ads (Q8)

**No ads in month 1.** At the end of month 1, review:
- If one or two Reels clearly beat the others organically (highest reach and shares), **boost them** with about 500–1,000 AED a month, targeted at business owners in Dubai.
- If nothing stands out, stay organic and fix the content first.

## 12. Goals and KPIs

**North star for months 1–3: follower growth** (Q15). Supporting signals tell us whether the growth is the right kind.

| | Month 1 | Month 2 | Month 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Followers** | 1,000 | 4,000 | 10,000 (stretch) |
| Mise-en-scène episodes | 2 | 4 | 6 (total) |
| Venues contacted (warm) | 20 | 40 | 60 (total) |
| Owner conversations | 3 | 6–8 | 10+ |
| Signed clients | 0–1 | 1–2 | 1 a month |

10k followers in 90 days from zero is ambitious. A realistic range is about 3k–6k, and 10k needs at least one Reel that breaks out. We judge the trend each month, not just the number.

**Supporting metrics (weekly, from Instagram Insights)**
- Shares per reach (sends) — the strongest growth signal
- Saves
- Profile visits → follows (conversion of the profile)
- Reel watch time / 3-second hold
- DMs with "SUIT" and WhatsApp conversations

## 13. What we won't do

- No other platforms this quarter.
- No puzzle grids, no follow-for-follow, no bought followers or engagement pods.
- No memes that break the look. One trend a week, in our style.
- No AI faces, voices or stock images (see Rules & AI policy).
- No hard selling: at most 1 in 5 posts sells.
- No posting client work before the client has published or approved it.
