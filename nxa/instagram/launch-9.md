# NxA — The First 9 Posts (soft launch)
<!-- version 1.0 · 2026-10-27 · reads: ../NxA-Brand-Identity.md, ../strategy/social-strategy.md, instagram-setup.md -->

We post these 9 before telling anyone. At 4 posts a week they take about 2.5 weeks. Then we announce on @natrvash, @avaizadpanah and @persianculinary.

**How to read the scripts**
- **Spoken** is what Ava says. Keep the meaning and say it in her own words; never read it off a phone.
- **On screen** is the burned-in text: Manrope Bold, Porcelain on Ink at 70%, lower third, 2 lines max.
- Navid's answers in Behind the frame are **suggestions only**. He should answer in his own words.
- Every Reel ends with the 1-second end card: the × on Ink, with no music sting.
- Fill in the `[brackets]` before filming.

## #1 — "We're Navid and Ava" · Ava's story · Reel 30–40 s

**Goal:** say who we are and what we do, and make people follow the duo.
**Format:** seated talking head, with b-roll cut in. Navid's camera or hands visible at the edge of the frame.

| Time | Shot | On screen | Spoken (Ava) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Ava to camera, medium close-up. Navid's lens visible at the frame edge. | We're Navid & Ava. | "We're Navid and Ava. He's behind the camera, I'm in front of it." |
| 3–8 s | Navid's hands adjusting a light, over-the-shoulder | He sees light, lines and space. | "He's a photographer and art director — he sees light, lines and space." |
| 8–13 s | Ava styling a table, hands and food | I see people, flavour and feeling. | "I'm a filmmaker, storyteller, cook and stylist — I see people, flavour and feeling." |
| 13–22 s | Frames from the Dubai interior project | Most places look amazing in real life… | "Together we noticed something. Most places in Dubai look amazing in real life — and look ordinary online." |
| 22–30 s | 35° × wipe to the best "after" frame | His frame. Her story. | "So we started NxA. He builds the frame, I tell the story." |
| 30–35 s | Back to Ava, a small smile; Navid lowers the camera, half in frame | Follow along. × | "This is the beginning. Follow along — we're dressing Dubai, one place at a time." |
| 35–36 s | End card | × | — |

## #4 — Ava's story: a past project · Reel 30–45 s

**Goal:** trust and storytelling. Ava tells one true story from past work.
**Format:** a walk-and-talk if you can return to the place; otherwise seated with b-roll.

**Story template (fill in):**
1. **Hook (0–3 s):** "The best shot of [project] happened by accident." / "This shoot almost didn't happen."
2. **Setting (3–10 s):** where, who, what they needed.
3. **The problem (10–20 s):** "[The light was gone / the client hated the first set / it rained in Dubai]."
4. **The turn (20–32 s):** what you two did. One specific, human detail.
5. **The result (32–40 s):** the best frame. "That's the frame on their wall now."
6. **Close:** "Real place. Real light. Real people." ×

**Caption shape:** 1 line of hook + 2 lines of story + 1 line of result.
