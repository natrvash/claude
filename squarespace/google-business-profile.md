## Description (750 characters max — this one is about 640)

Navid Atrvash Photography (Natrvash) is an architectural photography and film studio based in Dubai, run by award-winning architectural photographer Navid Atrvash and videographer Ava Izadpanah. We photograph and film architecture, interiors, hotels, restaurants and public spaces for architects, interior designers, developers and hospitality brands across the UAE and the Middle East. Navid, an M.Arch-trained architect, also provides high-end architectural retouching for professional photographers worldwide. Recognition includes the Architecture MasterPrize, the International Photography Awards and the European Photography Awards, with work published by ArchDaily and designboom.

## Services (Edit services › Add custom service)

- **Architectural photography** — Exteriors and interiors of buildings for architects, developers and publications, photographed with an architect's understanding of design intent.
- **Interior photography** — Residential and commercial interiors for interior designers and brands: light, material and detail as the designer meant them.
- **Hotel and hospitality photography** — Hotels, resorts, restaurants and cafés, from rooms and lobbies to food-and-space stories for marketing and press.
- **Architectural film** — Short films of finished projects for architecture firms, interior designers and cultural institutions, shot and edited by Ava Izadpanah.
- **Art direction and set styling** — Concept, styling and direction from the first site visit to the final frame.
- **Architectural retouching** — High-end retouching for professional architectural and interior photographers worldwide, finished to each photographer's own style.
