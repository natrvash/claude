Natrvash — Navid Atrvash & Ava Izadpanah

Architectural photography and film studio based in Dubai, working across the Middle East and beyond.

Natrvash photographs and films architecture, interiors and hospitality for architects, interior designers and brands, and retouches architectural images for photographers worldwide.

The studio is run by Navid Atrvash, an M.Arch-trained art director and photographer, and Ava Izadpanah, a stylist and architectural videographer trained in architecture and interior design. Together they read a space the way its designer meant it, and bring it to the image in stills and motion.
