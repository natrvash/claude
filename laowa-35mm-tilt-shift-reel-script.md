# Laowa 35mm Tilt-Shift BTS Reel — Final Script

A 30–35 second portrait video showing how I frame an interior with a shift lens: set the vanishing point, level the camera, shift to compose the frame, land the final image. Laowa may reshare it, so the lens should read clearly and the edit should feel premium.

| Item | Spec |

| Platform | Instagram video (feed / Reel) |
| Format | 2:3, 1080×1620 (matches the footage and photos); 4:5, 1080×1350 as an alternate crop |

| Audience | Photographers |
| Tone | Calm, warm, friendly — like one photographer talking to another, not an ad |

## Voiceover

I record the VO myself, one line per take, in two blocks with a 10-second music-only gap between them.

**Part 1**

> First, I set the vanishing point and level the camera. Then I use the shift to compose the frame.

**[10 s — music only]**

**Part 2**

> For me, a shift lens isn't just glass. It's a framing tool. Honestly, after this, a normal lens just feels limiting. It's like coffee, you know? Once you get used to the real thing… instant just doesn't do it anymore.

If the cut runs past 35 s, drop the line "Honestly, after this, a normal lens just feels limiting" first.

## Music

Calm, warm and minimal — it should feel like a quiet, well-lit room, not a trailer.

- **Instruments:** soft felt piano or warm ambient pads, maybe a light pulse; no vocals
- **Tempo:** slow, around 70–90 BPM
- **Energy:** low under the VO; a gentle lift during the 10 s music-only section (shift sequence); a soft swell when the final image lands; then settle
- **Avoid:** drops, big drums, cinematic hits, anything "epic" or corporate-upbeat
- **Reference feel:** modern neo-classical / minimal piano (Ólafur Arnalds or Nils Frahm style)
- **Mix:** duck music under the VO so every word is clear; bring it back up in the gap and at the end
- **Licensing:** use a properly licensed track (e.g. Artlist or Musicbed), not Instagram's in-app library — Laowa may reshare, and brand accounts can't always use in-app music

## Edit notes

- **Sound design:** keep the real click of the shift knob and the geared head under the music — it sells the precision
- **Pacing:** slow and confident; no fast transitions, glitches or zooms. Straight cuts or soft dissolves only
- **Grade:** match the final image — clean, warm, neutral whites, true verticals. No heavy LUT
- **Subtitles:** burned-in captions for the VO, small, clean sans-serif, lower-middle of frame, inside the 4:5 safe area
- **Text on screen:** captions only; no extra titles or graphics
- **Safe zones:** keep the lens, monitor detail and captions inside the central 4:5 area (about 135 px trimmed top and bottom of the 2:3 frame), so a 4:5 crop loses nothing; also clear of the IG UI overlays
- **Cover frame:** the final image, or the monitor shot with straight lines
- **Deliverable:** MP4, H.264, 1080×1620 (2:3) at 30 fps, plus a 1080×1350 (4:5) version if needed, and one version without captions
